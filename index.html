About Me

I am Kausar Ahmed. Currently working as a Research Assistant at the University of Liberal Arts Bangladesh (ULAB), where I also completed my B.Sc. in Computer Science and Engineering (CSE). I conduct NLP research with faculties on ongoing projects and mentor undergraduate capstone and thesis teams on research design, experimentation, and manuscript preparation.

My research interests are in Natural Language Processing (NLP) particularly in Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), Information Retrieval, Adaptive Retrieval Systems, and Trustworthy AI.

In my undergrad thesis, I explored lightweight, retraining-free ways to improve directing queries across multiple RAG pipelines under the supervision of Mr. Shah Jafor Sadeek Quaderi. My undergraduate capstone project, focused on developing LawB, an explainable, bilingual RAG-based Bangladeshi legal assistant.

I am also remotely collaborating with Dr. Prasanthi Sreekumari at the University of Louisiana at Monroe on research investigating Assertion-Based Probing for Sensitive Information Disclosure in Black-Box RAG Systems.

Research Projects

Sep 2026 – Present

Assertion-Based Probing for Sensitive Information Disclosure in Black-Box RAG Systems

Remote Research

In Collaboration with Dr. Prasanthi Sreekumari (University of Louisiana at Monroe)

Investigating whether false-premise queries can influence retrieval behavior or induce disclosure of protected information in black-box retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) systems. The research aims to develop a systematic probing methodology to assess the robustness of RAG systems against adversarial queries, with a focus on sensitive information leakage.

May – Sep 2026

MetricRouter: Adaptive Query Routing

Undergraduate Thesis

Supervised by Shah Jafor Sadeek Quaderi

Investigated lightweight query routing mechanisms for multi-pipeline legal RAG systems to mitigate the computational overhead of LLM classifiers. Proposed a metric-learning architecture coupled with a feedback-gated adaptation module to absorb user corrections without encoder retraining. Evaluated on a statutory law dataset, it achieved a 0.9420 macro-F1.

Jun 2025 – May 2026

LawB: An Interpretable RAG-Based Legal Assistant for Bangladesh’s Citizens

Undergraduate Capstone Project

Supervised by Shah Jafor Sadeek Quaderi

Investigated the mitigation of LLM hallucinations in bilingual legal systems by proposing D2RAG, a two-stage hierarchical retrieval architecture introducing metadata-scoped filtering, enriching the corpus for better semantic matching across highly correlated legal texts. The methodology demonstrated a 7% improvement in response completeness over baseline RAG.
